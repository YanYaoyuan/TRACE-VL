## Limits, validation and next experiment

- The starting LoRA was trained on eight-step reads. Larger reads introduce context-length/content-by-length shift; blank matching does not remove that interaction. The positive effects here do not establish generalization to another reader or live carried-state memory.
- Blank inputs are unusual; these contrasts isolate effects relative to this matched control, not every possible prompt effect. The retained bank and its original memory seed were not changed.
- LM timing is synchronized, unbatched and descriptive in the existing A100 environment. It includes the forward and option projection. **Do not compare packing times:** real twins construct layouts, blank twins reuse them. Raw preparation timings are kept only as execution diagnostics. Reference PyTorch causal-convolution/gated-delta fallbacks remain in this environment. No optimized deployment latency or utilization claim is made.
- The current useful practical reference is a larger frozen read budget. Learned selection/training must beat appropriately matched read-budget and trained random/spread controls; this diagnostic itself introduces no learned method.
- B0’s missing 40-versus-40 live-KV comparison remains necessary. Extra retained/read budget and a better writer are separate hypotheses.
